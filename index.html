Glidepath

Airmail, steered by hand

You are a letter, folded into a glider. Point your index finger at the screen and it flies where you point, over the rooftops and between the chimneys.

Best delivery—

How to play

Flying

Point
Aim your index finger at the screen. The glider climbs and dives to where you point; only height matters.
Pinch
In pinch to flap mode, touch your thumb to your index finger to flap instead.
Open palm
Show an open palm to hold the letter. Point again to resume.
Mouse
Move to steer. Click to post (and to flap in pinch mode).

Before posting

  • Turn off Reactions. Once the camera is on, open the green camera menu in the menu bar and switch Reactions off, or a thumbs-up sets off fireworks and confuses tracking.

  • Turn off Center Stage, Portrait and Studio Light in the same menu. Auto-framing breaks calibration.

  • Sit 30–50 cm from the camera in good light, and keep your hand large in the frame.
